# Native SAM direct-hit and two-ricochet probe

Use the private missionstarts.cpp substitution workflow with installed assets, isolated UserPath and Blackout fixture.sav. Restore missionstarts.cpp and rebuild the normal binary before installation. Production saves are never changed.

Local SAM missiles now perform only direct target damage on a hit within the native half-cell proximity tolerance. There is no ground or airborne splash and no terrain/wall area damage. A hit spawns a real travelling SAM missile toward the nearest legal visible hostile mobile target within a closed five-cell circle centered on the hit target. The initial missile may hit a building; subsequent targets are vehicles, infantry or aircraft. No actor may be hit twice within one chain. After at most two new missiles (three total hits), the chain stops. Misses and missing targets do not spawn ricochets.

Each chain records original raw projectile power, owner, remaining hops and visited native target IDs in an optional CNCCHN01 footer after CNCSAM03. Native missile layouts stay unchanged. The original raw power (currently25) is used for each new missile, so the existing anti-air impact modifier cannot compound through repeated air hits. Old in-flight missiles without a chain footer are initialized after pointer decoding using their stored power. Missile deletion clears the per-slot record. A chain keeps its initial allegiance if the firing SAM is subsequently destroyed. Allocation/terrain failure ends that chain without spawning other fire events or additional volleys.

Real BulletClass flight hits three targets in a line spaced four cells apart and leaves a fourth untouched. Ground target losses are25 each; airborne losses18 each under native anti-air/airborne armor rounding. The same results must hold when saved/reloaded after hit1 or hit2. Explicit impact tests require no immediate damage to the next target, a new travelling missile, and exact five-cell versus five-cell-plus-one-lepton boundary handling. Further checks cover misses, old saves with the optional footer removed, closer allied/neutral/hidden actors and buildings excluded from bounce selection, recycled bullet slots, and firing-SAM destruction. Enemy missiles retain stock damage and impact rules.

Run samupgrade_engine.cpp for unchanged range/sight/sustained cadence, samdurability_engine.cpp for HP/armor/paid repair/nearest-half targeting, and samdensity_engine.cpp for count/fraction boundaries. User preference is engine verification only; no UI launch or capture is required.
