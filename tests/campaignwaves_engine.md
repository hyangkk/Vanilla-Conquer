# Native growing reinforcement probe

Temporarily substitute campaignwaves_engine.cpp for missionstarts.cpp, build/copy a private diagnostic vanillatd, immediately restore missionstarts.cpp, and rebuild the normal release before installation. Use SDL dummy video/audio, null OpenAL, live assets via DataPath, and a private UserPath containing original starts/savegame.100–155 and 200–224. previous-progress.sav is an unmodified native progress save produced before the optional CNCWAV01 extension existed. Never write production user saves.

The probe loads all 81 starts. For pre-landing missions it first confirms no premature enemy wave, then drives real Logic.AI with original reinforcement/team/drop-zone triggers until the player lands; unrelated objective triggers are disabled only in the fixture. It checks first wave at 3 game minutes is 6, second at 6 minutes is 8, third at 9 minutes is 10. Counts must not compound under the separate enemy multiplier. All arrivals must be hostile and assigned hunt; Jurassic arrivals remain dinosaurs and the player's army count stays unchanged. The probe saves one tick before a deadline and immediately after delivery, reloads both, and verifies time and counts. Blackout additionally saves after 8 of a 10-unit arrival and resumes only the remaining 2 after loading. Previous-format progress gets a full three-minute grace rather than replaying overdue waves.

A separate 600-tick run drives actual newly spawned foot/vehicle AI and missiles, checking approach to the player. Skirmish, nonhuman player, victory, defeat, enemy elimination and elimination during a partially delivered wave are excluded. Empty/busy edge terrain uses the reachable landmass boundary; small or walled starts may need a closer legal approach. Allocation/terrain failures retain pending counts and retry, at most eight new units per simulation second. Original engine heap limits remain intact.

savegame.099 is a private camera/full-map-visibility fixture two game seconds before the real first arrival so the normal application can display actual units and its wave notice. No diagnostic visibility, trigger or HP changes are installed. A negative control with Campaign_Waves_AI disabled must fail the arrival assertions; restoring it must pass. Existing campaignenemies_engine.cpp must still pass its original multiplier/legacy-save checks.
